# wadi

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a wadi forms in an arid region by episodic flash floods.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique dry valley camera, minimal background.
Panel 1: dry desert surface with a shallow drainage line and sparse vegetation.
Panel 2: sudden heavy rain creates flash flood flow down the channel.
Panel 3: floodwater erodes the channel and transports gravel and sand.
Panel 4: completed dry wadi: a broad ephemeral stream channel with gravel bars after water disappears.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 건조 계곡", "2 집중호우", "3 홍수 침식", "4 와디". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: dry land tan, floodwater muddy blue-brown, erosion arrows blue-gray, gravel dark speckles.
Constraints: show episodic water then dry channel; not a permanent river, not a canyon with continuous flow, no city, no people.
