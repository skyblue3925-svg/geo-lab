# playa

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a playa forms in a closed arid basin through temporary flooding and evaporation.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, high oblique view of a closed desert basin.
Panel 1: closed basin surrounded by low mountains with no outlet.
Panel 2: rare rainfall and runoff carry fine sediment into the basin floor.
Panel 3: temporary shallow lake evaporates under dry climate, leaving salt and clay.
Panel 4: completed flat playa surface with pale salt crust and fine sediment.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 폐쇄 분지", "2 일시 유입", "3 증발", "4 플라야". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: basin tan, temporary water pale blue, salt crust white, sediment arrows brown.
Constraints: show closed basin and evaporation; not a permanent lake, not a beach, not a delta, no city, no people.
