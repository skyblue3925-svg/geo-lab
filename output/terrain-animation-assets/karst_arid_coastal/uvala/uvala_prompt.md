# uvala

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how an uvala forms when several dolines merge.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique overhead cutaway view of a limestone plateau.
Panel 1: several separate small dolines appear on jointed limestone.
Panel 2: groundwater solution and subsidence enlarge each doline.
Panel 3: neighboring dolines connect as their rims are dissolved and lowered.
Panel 4: completed uvala, a larger irregular closed basin made from merged dolines.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 여러 돌리네", "2 용식 확대", "3 함몰 결합", "4 우발라". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: limestone in pale gray, groundwater in turquoise, depression floors in red-brown terra rossa.
Constraints: make uvala much larger than a single doline; show multiple basins merging; not one collapse pit, not a lake basin, no city, no people.
