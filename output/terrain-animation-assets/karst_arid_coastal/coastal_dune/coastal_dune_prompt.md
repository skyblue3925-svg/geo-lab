# coastal_dune

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how coastal dunes form when dry beach sand is blown inland and trapped behind beach grasses, creating a dune ridge parallel to the shore.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D coastal geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique beach-to-inland camera.
Panel 1: waves and swash leave a wide dry sandy beach as the sand source.
Panel 2: onshore wind lifts and rolls dry sand from the beach toward the backshore.
Panel 3: beach grasses and small obstacles trap windblown sand into small embryo dunes behind the beach.
Panel 4: completed coastal dune ridge parallel to the shoreline, clearly behind the beach, with grasses stabilizing the dune and the sea still visible in front.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 모래 공급", "2 바람 이동", "3 식생 포획", "4 해안사구". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: beach sand light tan, sea blue, wind arrows blue-gray, vegetation green.
Constraints: show the dune ridge behind the beach, parallel to the coast; beach source and vegetation trapping must be obvious; not desert dunes far inland, not a beach berm only, not storm overwash, no city, no people.
