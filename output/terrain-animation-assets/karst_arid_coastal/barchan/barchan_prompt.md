# barchan

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a barchan dune forms and migrates under one prevailing wind direction.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique desert camera, minimal background.
Panel 1: limited sand supply on a hard desert surface with one prevailing wind.
Panel 2: sand gathers into a small mound with windward gentle slope and leeward slip face.
Panel 3: horns extend downwind as sand moves over the crest.
Panel 4: completed crescent-shaped barchan dune migrating downwind, horns pointing downwind.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 일정한 바람", "2 모래 집적", "3 뿔 발달", "4 바르한". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: sand warm tan, wind arrows blue-gray, slip face slightly darker.
Constraints: horns must point downwind; show limited sand; not a transverse dune, not a star dune, no oasis, no people, no city.
