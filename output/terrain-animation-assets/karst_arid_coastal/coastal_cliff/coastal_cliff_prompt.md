# coastal_cliff

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a coastal cliff forms and retreats by wave erosion.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique rocky coast camera with waves at the base.
Panel 1: waves attack the base of a rocky coast along a weakness zone.
Panel 2: a wave-cut notch grows at the foot of the slope.
Panel 3: the overhanging rock collapses and the cliff retreats landward.
Panel 4: completed steep coastal cliff with a wave-cut platform at low tide.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 파랑 침식", "2 해식동굴/노치", "3 붕괴 후퇴", "4 해식애". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: seawater blue, wave erosion arrows blue-white, bedrock gray, fresh collapse surfaces lighter.
Constraints: show wave-cut notch and platform; not river erosion, not tectonic fault scarp, no city, no people.
