# spit_lagoon

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a sand spit grows by longshore drift and almost closes a bay, leaving a distinct calm lagoon behind the sand barrier.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D coastal geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, high oblique aerial view of a bay coast.
Panel 1: waves approach the shore obliquely and longshore drift moves sand along a bay coast.
Panel 2: a narrow hooked sand spit grows from one side of the bay mouth, clearly projecting into the water.
Panel 3: the spit extends across most of the bay mouth and separates calm pale-blue water behind it from darker open sea outside.
Panel 4: completed spit-lagoon system: a continuous sandy barrier with a small tidal opening, a clearly enclosed shallow lagoon behind it, and waves only on the open-sea side.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 연안류", "2 사주 성장", "3 만 입구 차단", "4 석호". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: sea blue, lagoon pale turquoise, sand light tan, longshore drift arrows yellow/orange.
Constraints: lagoon must be visually obvious as enclosed calm water behind the sand barrier; show sand transport along coast; not a river delta, not tombolo to an island, no city, no people.
