# pediment

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a pediment forms as a gently sloping erosional rock surface at a mountain front in arid regions.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique mountain-front desert camera.
Panel 1: steep mountain front with bedrock exposed and small alluvial deposits at the base.
Panel 2: sheetwash and intermittent streams erode the mountain front and remove weathered material.
Panel 3: the mountain front retreats while a gently sloping bedrock surface widens.
Panel 4: completed pediment: broad low-angle erosional surface between mountain and basin.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 산록", "2 사면 침식", "3 후퇴", "4 페디먼트". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: bedrock gray-brown, sediment tan, runoff arrows blue-gray, slope surface subtly highlighted.
Constraints: show erosional bedrock surface, not an alluvial fan deposit, not a flat playa, no city, no people.
