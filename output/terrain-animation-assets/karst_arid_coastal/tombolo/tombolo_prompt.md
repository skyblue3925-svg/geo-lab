# tombolo

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a tombolo forms when wave refraction and longshore drift connect an island to the mainland.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D coastal geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, high oblique aerial view of mainland coast, nearshore island, and waves.
Panel 1: offshore island causes wave refraction and a low-energy shadow zone behind it.
Panel 2: longshore drift moves sand toward the sheltered zone.
Panel 3: a sand bar grows from the mainland toward the island and from the island side.
Panel 4: completed tombolo: narrow sand bar connects island and mainland.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 파랑 굴절", "2 퇴적 집중", "3 모래톱 성장", "4 육계사주". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: sea blue, sand tan, wave rays white, sediment arrows yellow/orange.
Constraints: clearly connect an island to mainland; not a spit closing a lagoon, not a bridge, no city, no people.
