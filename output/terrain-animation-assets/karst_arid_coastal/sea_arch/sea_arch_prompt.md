# sea_arch

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a sea arch forms from wave erosion along cracks in a headland.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D coastal geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique rocky headland camera with waves.
Panel 1: waves attack cracks and joints on both sides of a rocky headland.
Panel 2: sea caves form and deepen along the weaknesses.
Panel 3: caves connect through the headland as wave erosion continues.
Panel 4: completed sea arch with waves passing through the opening.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 절리 침식", "2 해식동굴", "3 관통", "4 해식아치". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: seawater blue, wave impact arrows white-blue, rock gray/brown, cave interiors dark.
Constraints: show headland and wave erosion; not a river natural bridge, not a human-made arch, no city, no people.
