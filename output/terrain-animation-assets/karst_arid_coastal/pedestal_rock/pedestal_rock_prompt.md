# pedestal_rock

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a pedestal rock or mushroom rock forms by stronger abrasion near the ground.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same close oblique desert rock camera, minimal background.
Panel 1: isolated rock block with harder upper layer and softer lower layer in a windy desert.
Panel 2: sand-laden wind abrades the lower part more intensely near the ground.
Panel 3: the base narrows while the upper cap remains wider.
Panel 4: completed mushroom-shaped pedestal rock with narrow neck and broad cap.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 암석 노출", "2 모래 마식", "3 하부 약화", "4 버섯바위". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: rock gray/tan, wind arrows blue-gray, sand particles gold, eroded lower zone highlighted.
Constraints: show abrasion concentrated near ground; not water erosion, not a karst tower, not a hoodoo forest, no people, no city.
