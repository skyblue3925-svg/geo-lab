# tower_karst

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how tower karst forms by long-term differential solution of limestone.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique landscape camera over a limestone plain.
Panel 1: a broad jointed limestone surface begins to dissolve in humid conditions.
Panel 2: surrounding limestone is lowered by solution while more resistant blocks remain higher.
Panel 3: residual limestone masses become isolated as the plain widens.
Panel 4: completed tower karst with steep limestone towers rising from a low plain.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 용식 시작", "2 주변 저하", "3 잔구 분리", "4 탑 카르스트". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: limestone towers pale gray, low plain green/red-brown, groundwater/solution arrows turquoise.
Constraints: show residual limestone towers after solution; not volcanic plugs, not sand dunes, not wind-carved pillars, no city, no people.
