# karren

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how karren grooves form on exposed limestone by rainwater solution.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, close oblique view of exposed limestone pavement, minimal background.
Panel 1: bare limestone surface with small cracks and joints.
Panel 2: slightly acidic rainwater flows along cracks and tiny surface channels.
Panel 3: solution widens grooves and sharpens ridges between them.
Panel 4: completed karren limestone pavement with visible grooves, runnels, and sharp clints.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 노출 석회암", "2 빗물 용식", "3 홈 확대", "4 카렌". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: limestone pale gray, water arrows blue, dissolved grooves dark gray shadows.
Constraints: show small surface-scale grooves; not large valleys, not lava cracks, not desert yardangs, no city, no people.
