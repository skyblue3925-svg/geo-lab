# transverse_dune

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how transverse dunes form where sand supply is abundant and wind direction is steady.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique desert camera looking across dune ridges.
Panel 1: broad sandy surface with abundant loose sand and one prevailing wind.
Panel 2: sand gathers into low ripples perpendicular to wind direction.
Panel 3: ripples grow into parallel dune ridges with slip faces on the lee side.
Panel 4: completed transverse dune field: long parallel ridges crossing the wind direction.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 많은 모래", "2 모래 물결", "3 능선 성장", "4 횡사구". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: sand tan/gold, wind arrows blue-gray, lee slip faces darker.
Constraints: show parallel ridges, not crescent dunes, not star dunes; wind should be one direction; no people, no city.
