# mesa_butte

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how mesas and buttes form by differential erosion of horizontal sedimentary layers.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique arid plateau camera with horizontal strata.
Panel 1: broad plateau with resistant caprock over softer sedimentary layers.
Panel 2: running water and weathering erode valley sides and remove softer layers.
Panel 3: the plateau is dissected into flat-topped remnants; larger remnants become mesas.
Panel 4: continued erosion leaves smaller steep-sided buttes beside larger mesas.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 수평층", "2 차별 침식", "3 메사", "4 뷰트". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: caprock dark brown, softer layers tan/red bands, erosion arrows blue-gray.
Constraints: show flat tops and horizontal strata; not volcanoes, not dunes, not karst towers, no people, no city.
