# karst_doline

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a doline forms in limestone karst.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique cutaway view of limestone ground, minimal background.
Panel 1: rainwater enters joints and bedding planes in limestone.
Panel 2: acidic groundwater dissolves limestone and enlarges underground drainage paths.
Panel 3: the surface slowly subsides and a small closed depression grows.
Panel 4: completed doline with a bowl-shaped closed depression, terra rossa soil, and underground drainage.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 빗물 침투", "2 석회암 용식", "3 함몰 확대", "4 돌리네". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: limestone in pale gray, groundwater in turquoise blue, terra rossa in red-brown, solution arrows in blue.
Constraints: show chemical solution and underground drainage; not a meteor crater, not a volcanic crater, not a simple landslide hole, no city, no people.
