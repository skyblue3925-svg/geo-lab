# ria_coast

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a ria coast forms when sea level rises and floods a branching river valley system.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D coastal geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, high oblique aerial terrain view of a dendritic river valley coast.
Panel 1: branching river valleys cut into a coastal landscape before flooding.
Panel 2: sea level rises or the coast subsides after the valleys are carved.
Panel 3: seawater enters the branching valleys and floods the lower courses.
Panel 4: completed ria coast with irregular branching bays and peninsulas.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 하천 계곡", "2 해수면 상승", "3 계곡 침수", "4 리아스식 해안". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: seawater dark blue, land green/brown, sea-level arrows blue, former river valleys subtle.
Constraints: show branching drowned river valleys; not a glacial fjord, not a delta, not a straight coast, no city, no people.
