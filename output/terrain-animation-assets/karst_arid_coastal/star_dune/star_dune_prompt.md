# star_dune

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a star dune forms under winds from multiple directions.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, high oblique desert camera, minimal background.
Panel 1: abundant sand on a desert surface with wind arrows from several directions.
Panel 2: a central mound grows as shifting winds pile sand from different sides.
Panel 3: several arms radiate from the central peak, each with slip faces.
Panel 4: completed star dune with three or more radiating arms and a high central summit.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 다방향 바람", "2 중심 집적", "3 팔 발달", "4 성상사구". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: sand tan/gold, multi-direction wind arrows blue-gray, shadowed slip faces darker.
Constraints: show multiple wind directions and radiating arms; not a barchan crescent, not transverse parallel ridges, no people, no city.
