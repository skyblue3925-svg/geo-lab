# u_valley

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a V-shaped valley becomes a U-shaped valley by glacial erosion.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique valley cross-section camera, minimal background.
Panel 1: a narrow V-shaped river valley in a high mountain setting begins accumulating snow.
Panel 2: a glacier fills the valley and slowly moves downslope.
Panel 3: the glacier erodes both the valley floor and side walls at the same time, widening and deepening the cross-section.
Panel 4: completed U-shaped valley with a broad flat floor and steep valley walls after the glacier retreats.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 V자곡", "2 빙하 이동", "3 넓은 침식", "4 U자곡". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: glacial ice in translucent blue, erosion arrows in blue, exposed rock in gray, snow/ice subtle.
Constraints: show transition from V to U cross-section; glacial erosion must affect both floor and walls; no river-only valley, no fjord seawater, no people, no city, no fantasy style, no excessive snowstorm.
