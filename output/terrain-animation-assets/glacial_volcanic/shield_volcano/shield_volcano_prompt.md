# shield_volcano

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a shield volcano forms from low-viscosity basaltic lava.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same low oblique volcanic landscape camera, minimal background.
Panel 1: basaltic magma rises through a vent.
Panel 2: thin fluid lava erupts gently and flows far from the vent.
Panel 3: repeated lava flows spread widely and build broad gentle slopes.
Panel 4: completed wide low shield volcano with very gentle profile.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 마그마 공급", "2 묽은 용암", "3 넓은 확산", "4 순상화산". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: lava in red-orange, tectonic/magma arrows in muted orange, cooled basalt in dark gray.
Constraints: show broad low profile; not steep stratovolcano; no explosive ash column, no city, no people, no fantasy style.
