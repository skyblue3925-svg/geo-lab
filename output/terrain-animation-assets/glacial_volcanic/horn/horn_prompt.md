# horn

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a horn forms when several cirques erode a mountain peak from different sides.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique mountain peak camera, minimal background.
Panel 1: a broad mountain peak with several small cirques around it.
Panel 2: glaciers in three or more cirques erode inward toward the summit.
Panel 3: ridges between cirques narrow and the peak becomes sharper.
Panel 4: completed sharp pyramidal horn peak with surrounding cirques.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 여러 권곡", "2 다방향 침식", "3 정상 첨예화", "4 호른". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: glacial erosion arrows in blue from multiple directions, exposed rock in gray, ice/snow subtle.
Constraints: show multiple cirques converging; do not make it a volcano; do not show one-direction erosion only; no people, no city, no fantasy style.
