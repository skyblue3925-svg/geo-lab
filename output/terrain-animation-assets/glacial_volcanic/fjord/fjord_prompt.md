# fjord

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a fjord forms when a glacial U-shaped valley is flooded by seawater.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique coastal valley camera looking inland, minimal background.
Panel 1: a deep U-shaped glacial valley with steep walls after strong glacial erosion.
Panel 2: the glacier retreats and sea level rises near the coast.
Panel 3: seawater floods into the deep glacial valley.
Panel 4: completed fjord: long narrow deep inlet with steep valley walls and dark blue seawater.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 U자곡", "2 빙하 후퇴", "3 해수 유입", "4 피오르". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: ice in pale blue, seawater in dark blue, change arrows in gray, exposed rock in gray/green.
Constraints: show glacial valley first and seawater second; not a ria coast, not a river delta, no people, no city, no fantasy style.
