# lava_plateau

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a lava plateau forms from fissure eruptions.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same high oblique landscape camera over a broad plain with fissures, minimal background.
Panel 1: long cracks open in the crust across a broad region.
Panel 2: low-viscosity lava floods out from the fissures and spreads widely.
Panel 3: repeated lava sheets cover older valleys and level the surface.
Panel 4: completed broad flat lava plateau with layered basalt edges.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 균열", "2 용암 유출", "3 반복 피복", "4 용암대지". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: fresh lava in red-orange, cooled basalt in dark gray, plateau surface in brown/gray.
Constraints: show wide fissure eruption, not a central cone; broad flat plateau, no single volcano summit, no city, no people, no fantasy style.
