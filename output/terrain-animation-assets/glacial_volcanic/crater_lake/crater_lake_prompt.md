# crater_lake

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a crater lake forms in a volcanic crater or caldera basin.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique volcanic basin camera, minimal background.
Panel 1: an empty volcanic crater or collapsed basin remains after eruption.
Panel 2: rainfall, snowmelt, and groundwater begin entering the enclosed basin.
Panel 3: water level rises because drainage is limited.
Panel 4: completed crater lake filling the volcanic basin.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 분지 형성", "2 물 유입", "3 수위 상승", "4 화구호". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: water in clear blue, inflow arrows in blue, volcanic rim in dark gray/brown.
Constraints: show volcanic rim/basin clearly; not a normal river lake; no city, no people, no fantasy style, no excessive labels.
