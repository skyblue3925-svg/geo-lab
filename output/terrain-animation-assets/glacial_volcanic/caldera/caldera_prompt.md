# caldera

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a caldera forms by collapse after a large eruption.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique volcano cross-section camera, minimal background.
Panel 1: a large magma chamber grows beneath a volcanic edifice.
Panel 2: a massive eruption empties much of the magma chamber.
Panel 3: the roof above the emptied chamber collapses downward.
Panel 4: completed broad caldera basin with a raised rim; optional lake only subtle.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 마그마 방", "2 대분출", "3 함몰", "4 칼데라". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: magma in orange-red, collapse arrows in gray, volcanic rock in gray/brown.
Constraints: show collapse after eruption; caldera must be much wider than a simple crater; not erosion basin, no city, no people, no fantasy style.
