# arete

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how an arête forms between two glacial cirques.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique view of two adjacent cirques and the ridge between them, minimal background.
Panel 1: two neighboring cirque glaciers begin forming on opposite sides of a ridge.
Panel 2: both glaciers erode inward toward the ridge from each side.
Panel 3: the ridge narrows and becomes steep as both sides are carved.
Panel 4: completed knife-edge arête separating two glacial basins.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 두 권곡", "2 양쪽 침식", "3 능선 축소", "4 아레트". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: erosion arrows in blue on both sides, ridge in gray exposed rock, ice in pale blue.
Constraints: show a long sharp ridge, not a peak; distinguish from horn; no people, no city, no fantasy style, no excessive arrows.
