# stratovolcano

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a stratovolcano forms from viscous lava and explosive tephra layers.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique volcano cross-section camera, minimal background.
Panel 1: viscous magma rises beneath a central vent.
Panel 2: explosive eruption releases ash and pyroclastic material.
Panel 3: alternating lava flows and ash/tephra layers build a steep cone.
Panel 4: completed tall steep stratovolcano with visible layered structure.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 점성 마그마", "2 폭발 분출", "3 층상 축적", "4 성층화산". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: magma/lava in red-orange, ash in gray, layers in alternating dark and tan bands.
Constraints: show layered cone and steep slope; not shield volcano; no giant fantasy eruption, no people, no city, no excessive smoke obscuring structure.
