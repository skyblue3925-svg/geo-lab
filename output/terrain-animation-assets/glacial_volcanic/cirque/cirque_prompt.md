# cirque

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: high-school terrain formation storyboard
Primary request: Create a 4-panel horizontal educational storyboard showing how a cirque forms near a mountain headwall.
Audience: Korean high school geography students.
Style/medium: realistic textbook-style 3D geomorphology diagram, clean classroom infographic, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: four panels arranged left to right, same oblique overhead camera on a mountain summit hollow, minimal background.
Panel 1: snow accumulates in a small hollow high on a mountainside.
Panel 2: the snow compacts into a small glacier that begins rotating and sliding.
Panel 3: plucking and abrasion carve a deeper semicircular bowl with a steep headwall.
Panel 4: completed cirque with a bowl-shaped hollow, steep back wall, and possible small tarn.
Text: use only very short Korean labels: "1 적설", "2 소빙하", "3 권곡 침식", "4 권곡". Keep labels thin and legible.
Color/overlay rules: ice in translucent blue, erosion in blue, exposed bedrock in gray, snow in white.
Constraints: show a bowl-shaped high mountain hollow; not a volcanic crater; not a lowland basin; no people, no city, no fantasy style, no excessive labels.
