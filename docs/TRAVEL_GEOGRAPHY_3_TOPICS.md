# Travel Geography 3-Topic Lesson Pack (Geo-lab)

## Topic 1: River Landforms and Basin Thinking

- Learning goal: Explain how slope, discharge, and sediment shape V-valley, meander, and delta.
- Geo-lab pages:
  - `pages/1_📖_Gallery.py`: quick visual examples
  - `pages/3_🧪_Lab.py`: variable control experiment
- Class activity (40-50 min):
  - Compare 3 landforms in Gallery
  - Change one variable in Lab (erosion or uplift)
  - Record shape change and reason
- Assessment:
  - Short CER: "Which factor most strongly controlled shape change?"
  - Exit ticket with one evidence screenshot

## Topic 2: Coastal Change and Human Risk

- Learning goal: Analyze cliff retreat, spit/lagoon growth, and tombolo from process view.
- Geo-lab pages:
  - `pages/1_📖_Gallery.py`: coastal set
  - `pages/4_🔬_Research.py`: scenario and data-style discussion
- Class activity (40-50 min):
  - Observe two coastal settings
  - Predict hazard zones (erosion/flooding)
  - Test prediction with scenario run
- Assessment:
  - Hazard map sketch + 3-line justification
  - Peer review with one counter-example

## Topic 3: Climate-Human Interaction in Regional Systems

- Learning goal: Connect climate signals with hydrology, geomorphic response, and planning choices.
- Geo-lab pages:
  - `pages/5_☁️_Climate.py`: climate module
  - `pages/3_🧪_Lab.py`: human intervention test
- Class activity (40-50 min):
  - Pick one climate condition
  - Compare "no intervention" vs "intervention" outcome
  - Propose policy or local adaptation plan
- Assessment:
  - 1-page policy memo: risk, evidence, decision, limitation

## Teacher-ready flow (3 weeks)

1. Week 1: River systems and basin experiment
2. Week 2: Coastal morphology and hazard inference
3. Week 3: Climate-human coupling and adaptation memo

## Fast rubric (10 points)

- Concept accuracy: 4
- Evidence use (Geo-lab capture + data statement): 3
- Reasoning clarity: 2
- Reflection on limits/uncertainty: 1
